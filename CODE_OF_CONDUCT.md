# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Hyperblaze is an open source project for people who care about build tooling, Rust, and developer experience. We are committed to making project spaces friendly, safe, and useful for contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still taking impact seriously.
- Keep technical criticism focused on ideas, code, and tradeoffs.
- Give newcomers enough context to participate.
- Respect maintainers' time and decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, or abuse of project communication channels are not acceptable. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events, and any space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to Ashutosh Kumar Singh at ashutoshkumarsingh0x@gmail.com. Please include relevant links, screenshots, dates, and the names or handles involved where possible.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, block accounts, or take other moderation action needed to protect the community.

## Alignment

Hyperblaze follows the spirit of the Rust Project and Rust Foundation community standards: technical excellence works best when paired with respectful collaboration.
